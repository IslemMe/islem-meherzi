My research focuses on leveraging health data and advanced analytical methods to address critical questions in global health research and policy. I apply bibliometric and scientometric approaches to understand research patterns, collaboration networks, and evidence gaps in healthcare.

Current projects examine research-burden alignment, international collaboration patterns, and methodological innovations in health data analysis.

Research Directions

  • Bibliometric Analysis – applying quantitative methods to analyze research output patterns and impact
  • Scientometric Research – measuring and analyzing scientific literature and research productivity
  • Global Health Research – examining research priorities and their alignment with disease burden
  • Research Collaboration Networks – mapping and analyzing international research partnerships
  • Health Data Analytics – utilizing large-scale health datasets for research insights
  • Evidence-Based Policy – supporting health policy decisions through data-driven research
  • Public Health Data Science – applying advanced analytics to population health challenges
  • Research Methodology Innovation – developing new approaches for health research analysis
  • Health Research Equity – investigating disparities and gaps in global health research
